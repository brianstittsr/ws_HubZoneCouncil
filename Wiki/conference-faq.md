# 2026 National HUBZone Conference FAQ

## When and where is the conference?

The 2026 National HUBZone Conference takes place July 21–22, 2026 at the Westfields Marriott, 14750 Conference Center Dr, Chantilly, VA 20151.

## What is the pre-event?

The HUBZone Golf Invitational is scheduled for July 20, 2026.

## What are the conference hours?

The conference runs from 8:00 AM to 5:30 PM each day.

## What is the conference theme?

The theme is "HUBZone on the Rise — Building America's Next 250 Years."

## Who should attend?

The conference is designed for HUBZone-certified small businesses, federal agency representatives, prime contractors, economic development professionals, and policymakers.

## What tracks are offered?

- Federal Contracting Access
- Policy & Advocacy
- Networking & Matchmaking
- Supply Chain & Manufacturing
- Workforce Development
- Innovation & Growth

## What are the executive think tanks?

- Workforce Development & Access to Capital
- Defense and National Security
- Energy, Infrastructure & Critical Minerals
- Manufacturing and Supply Chain
- Agriculture and Healthcare

## How much are tickets?

- General Admission Member: $650
- General Admission Non-Member: $750
- Exhibitor Member: $2,150
- Exhibitor Non-Member: $2,550
- Government Registration: $250
